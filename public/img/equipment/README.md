# Equipment illustrations

These local SVG illustrations are custom, stylized product-category artwork rather than manufacturer photographs. They are drawn in the site's dark navy and gold visual style and kept at a 1:1 ratio.

The carousel image filenames are configured in `public/assets/app.js` under `equipmentConfig.categories`. To replace an illustration with an approved product photo, save an optimized square WebP or SVG in this folder, update the matching filename in that configuration, and retain the visible card label. Keep the image focused on the device against a clean, high-contrast background.
